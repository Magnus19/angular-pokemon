Pokemon Gym Leader Guide

Explore the history, culture, and champions of Kanto and Johto as you move through the regions and their iconic gym leaders.

Kanto

Kanto is the original region of the Pokemon journey, where the first trainers began their path to become champions. It is home to the first generation's iconic gyms, classic rivalries, and the great challenge of reaching the Indigo League.

Johto

Johto is a region rich in history and tradition, known for its lush forests, old shrines, and strong connection to the legacy of earlier generations. It is also where many trainers test their skills against a diverse set of gym leaders and evolving challenges.
